Are you ready to begin your journey with Jesus Christ? We'll walk with you step by step. This is a place of grace, open to all who seek.

The 7-Step Journey

A guided path designed to support your spiritual growth at every stage. We are here with resources, community, and prayer.

Step 01

The Invitation

Understanding the call and opening your heart to the possibility of a relationship with God. It starts with a simple 'yes'.

Step 02

Belief

Building the foundation of faith and understanding the core tenets of Christianity.

Step 03

Repentance

Turning away from old paths and embracing the grace offered for a new beginning.

Step 04

Confession

Declaring your faith publicly and finding strength in shared vulnerability within the community.
