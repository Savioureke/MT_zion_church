## Brand & Style

The design system is anchored in a philosophy of "Modern Sanctuary." It balances the weight of tradition with the accessibility of modern digital experiences. The visual narrative should evoke a sense of peace, intentionality, and spiritual elevation.

The style is a blend of **Minimalism** and **High-Contrast Editorial**. It utilizes generous whitespace to give content room to breathe, suggesting a quiet, contemplative space. Large-scale, high-quality photography acts as a window into the community and the divine, while sharp, elegant typography provides the authoritative voice of heritage. 

Key attributes:
- **Welcoming:** Open layouts and warm tones.
- **Grounded:** Solid charcoal foundations and stable grid structures.
- **Inspiring:** Gold accents and light-filled imagery.
- **Professional:** Precise alignment and sophisticated type pairings.

## Colors

The palette is derived from the natural transition of a sunset over earth—transitioning from deep, grounded shadows to radiant, divine light.

- **Primary (Gold - #D4AF37):** Used for primary actions, progress indicators in learning paths, and subtle accents. It represents illumination and the "Gold Standard" of spiritual growth.
- **Secondary (Deep Charcoal - #2C2C2B):** Used for high-contrast backgrounds, primary text, and footer areas. This provides the "grounded" feel necessary for a serious ministry.
- **Tertiary (Parchment - #F9F5F0):** The primary background color for content sections. It is softer than pure white, reducing eye strain and feeling more organic/natural.
- **Neutral (Stone - #706F6C):** Used for secondary text, borders, and UI elements like inactive icons or metadata labels.

**Color Application:**
Use dark charcoal backgrounds with gold and parchment text for high-impact hero sections or "deep study" modes. Use parchment backgrounds with charcoal text for high-legibility reading experiences.

## Typography

This design system uses a high-contrast type pairing to bridge the gap between historical reverence and modern clarity.

- **Headings (Libre Caslon Text):** This serif font carries weight and authority. It should be used for all editorial titles, sermon titles, and major section headers. It is best presented in its regular weight to allow the letterforms' natural elegance to show.
- **Body & UI (Hanken Grotesk):** A sharp, contemporary sans-serif. It is used for long-form reading, descriptions, and all functional UI elements. It ensures the platform feels like a modern tool rather than just a static document.

**Hierarchy Rules:**
- Use `label-caps` for eyebrows (small text above headers) to categorize content (e.g., "SERMON SERIES").
- Large display sizes should use negative letter-spacing for a tighter, more "designed" editorial feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is a **Fixed-Width Grid** on desktop to maintain editorial control, transitioning to a **Fluid Grid** on mobile.

- **Whitespace:** Use aggressive vertical spacing (`section-gap`) to separate major thematic blocks. This prevents the "cluttered" feel of traditional information-heavy ministry sites.
- **The 12-Column Grid:** Use a standard 12-column grid for desktop. Content should frequently be "offset"—for example, a headline spanning 6 columns on the left with body text occupying columns 8 through 11. This asymmetry creates a sophisticated, modern look.
- **Asymmetric Balance:** Align images to one edge of the container while keeping text within the grid to create a sense of movement and "growth."

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a grounded and professional aesthetic, this design system avoids heavy shadows or neomorphic effects. Instead, depth is created through **Tonal Layering** and **Image-as-Base**.

- **Stacked Surfaces:** Use subtle shifts in background color (e.g., a white card on a Parchment background) to indicate interactable areas.
- **Image Overlays:** Content cards often sit slightly overlapping the edge of a high-quality photo, creating a physical sense of depth.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Use 1px borders in `Neutral (Stone)` at 20% opacity for input fields and card boundaries to keep the UI "light."
- **Focus States:** When an element is active or focused, use a subtle 2px solid `Primary (Gold)` border rather than a shadow.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft (0.25rem)**. 

While the brand is modern, overly rounded "bubble" corners would feel too casual. The soft 4px corner radius provides a touch of approachability while maintaining the structural integrity and "architectural" feel of a professional institution. 

- **Buttons:** Use soft-cornered rectangles. Avoid full pills except for specific interactive tags/chips.
- **Media Containers:** Video and audio players should use the 4px radius.
- **Learning Path Connectors:** Lines connecting stages in a "Growth Journey" should be thin (1px) and straight, reinforcing a clear, direct path.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Gold background with Charcoal text. No shadow.
- **Secondary:** Transparent with a 1px Charcoal border.
- **Ghost:** Charcoal text with no border, used for secondary navigation.

### Audio & Video Players
- **Audio:** A slim, horizontal bar with a Charcoal background. Play/Pause buttons in Gold. Use a simplified waveform visualization in Stone.
- **Video:** Large thumbnails with the Gold "Play" icon centered. Use a subtle gradient overlay at the bottom for title legibility.

### Growth Journey (Learning Paths)
- **Step Indicator:** A vertical 1px line connecting nodes. Completed nodes are Gold circles; current nodes are Gold outlines; future nodes are Stone outlines.
- **Progress:** Use a thin Gold progress bar at the top of lesson cards.

### Input Fields
- Flat design. Underline-only inputs or very light grey (Parchment-tinted) boxes. Typography within inputs should be Hanken Grotesk.

### Cards
- **Editorial Cards:** No border, large image top-aligned, heading in Libre Caslon.
- **Content Cards:** Parchment background, 1px Stone border at low opacity, used for structured data like sermon notes or calendar events.
